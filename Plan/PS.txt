Why I think this is a strong demo for XL Ventures
There is another reason I like this idea.
You're applying for an internship at XL Ventures.
If your demo shows how their own analysts or investment team could use an Agentic AI platform to speed up startup evaluation, it becomes immediately relatable to the judges.
At the same time, during the presentation you can say:
"Although our demonstration is in Venture Capital, the platform itself is domain-agnostic. By replacing the domain-specific agents and knowledge sources, the same architecture supports loan approvals, procurement decisions, insurance claims, hiring, and other enterprise decision workflows."
That statement aligns very closely with the hackathon's emphasis on a reusable Agentic Decision Intelligence Platform rather than a one-off application.

One architectural improvement I'd recommend
Since this is an Agentic AI hackathon, don't have every analysis agent run on every pitch deck.
Instead, let your Planner Agent decide dynamically.
For example:
If the pitch deck has financial projections, invoke the Financial Agent.
If founder profiles are present, invoke the Founder Agent.
If customer traction is mentioned, invoke the Market Agent.
If legal or compliance information is missing, invoke a Missing Information Agent.
That demonstrates dynamic orchestration, which is one of the key judging criteria, instead of a fixed sequential pipeline. It will make your architecture feel much more "agentic" and aligned with the problem statement.
